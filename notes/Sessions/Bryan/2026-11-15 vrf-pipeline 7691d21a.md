# PBR textures for generated ships: MAK effect maps, confirmed with a probe

**Asked:** generated models have colour only, while stock models (e.g. the Nimitz) are textured and VR-Forces
uses PBR. Plan and explore how the model generator can match the stock models' visual fidelity. Record the
probe's observations in the docs and notes.

## Done
- **How VR-Forces does PBR** (manuals and install, read-only):
  - Maps are found **by file name** beside the colour texture (AddContentToMAK 12.4), e.g. `X.png` +
    `X_NML.png` (normal) + one composite. The composite lists its codes in red, green, blue order (12.4.1).
  - The stock PBR ships (Gerald Ford, Arleigh Burke) use `X_MTL_GLS_AO.png` (metal, gloss, ambient occlusion),
    4096², and their model files reference only `X.png`.
  - The shaders light with GGX: gloss = 1 − roughness, and the normal's tangents come from screen-space
    derivatives, so meshes need none.
  - The stock Nimitz is older: colour textures only (17 atlases at 4096²), with 6 levels of detail and a damage
    switch.
- **Plan agreed** (3 decisions below). Steps:
  - (0) a probe model to confirm the format;
  - (1) the importer writes effect maps;
  - (2) the generator bakes textures;
  - (3) the editor gets a bake step, a textured viewer and exports that use it.
- **Importer** (vrf-pipeline, copied to `mak/pipeline`):
  - `pipeline/effectmaps.py` turns a glTF material's normal, metallic-roughness and occlusion maps into
    `<texture>_NML.png` and `<texture>_MTL_GLS_AO.png`;
  - `pipeline/png.py` is a standard-library PNG reader/writer. It is byte-exact on Blender's PNGs, but takes
    4-24 s per 4096² map;
  - new checks: `texture-jpeg-effect`, `flt-texture-missing`, `flt-effect-maps`, `effect-maps-flt-only`;
    inspect reads JPEG sizes;
  - MAK's `medfTool` compresses our `_NML` files to two-channel BC5, as on stock ships.
- **Probe** (`scripts/make_pbr_probe.py` in vrf-pipeline): a barge with eight 4 m panels (A-H), each testing
  one thing. Bryan imported it into the SOE. Measured from the side-view screenshot (open question Q23,
  resolved):
  - maps load by name on a plain OpenFlight model: the metal panel renders dark and reflective (84 against 118);
  - ambient occlusion works, but subtly (bands about 112 / 117; it only darkens ambient light);
  - the normal map's +Y points to the top of the image (glTF's convention), so no flip is needed;
  - a plain RGB normal map is read from green; the copy of Y in alpha does no harm and is kept;
  - textures and face colours render the same for the same values: one colour space;
  - in that scene, black renders at about 51 and white at about 203.

## Decisions
- **Hybrid bake:** procedural finishes (non-skid deck, hull plating, streaks, edge wear) plus baked ambient
  occlusion, markings and hull numbers, in unique atlases per ship.
- **Bake as a separate step:** builds stay fast and flat; the bake runs on demand and before a MAK export, and
  is cached per version.
- **Stock resolution:** 4096² for hull and deck, 2048² for superstructure and details, configurable.
- Normal maps are written RGBA with Y in both green and alpha (the probe showed either works).
- Not now: emissive (`_EMM`) and SensorFX maps, effect maps on the FBX route, levels of detail and damage
  geometry.
